## Research references for the expanded edition

- [The Prompt Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608): a research taxonomy; terminology and categories are not universal standards.
- [Chain-of-Thought Prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903): reasoning demonstrations in evaluated research settings.
- [Least-to-Most Prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10625): decomposition followed by ordered subproblem solving.
- [Self-Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171): sampled solution paths and answer aggregation.
- [ReAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629): combining reasoning and actions in interactive workflows.

The expanded edition adds 34 detailed technique guides, deeper sections in all 15 introductory lessons, application concepts, and a shot-count comparison lab. Expected outputs are illustrative reference behavior, not fabricated experiment results. No numerical performance claims are inferred from the papers for this repository’s exercises.
