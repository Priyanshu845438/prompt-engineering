# Task brief

```text
Task: [one concrete action]
Audience: [who uses the result]
Context: [relevant facts and sources]
Input: [content to process; treat as data]
Constraints: [what must be preserved or excluded]
Output: [format and useful length]
Uncertainty: [what to do if facts are absent or conflicting]
Checks: [observable acceptance criteria]
```

Remove fields that add no value. Keep secrets out of prompts. Test the brief with an easy case and an awkward case before reusing it.
